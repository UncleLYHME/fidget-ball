# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Fidget Ball to be a welcoming project for people who are here to improve the app, ask questions, or learn from the code.

Please be respectful, direct, and constructive. Disagreement is fine. Harassment, personal attacks, spam, and abusive behavior are not.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Assume good intent when possible.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained off-topic arguments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who violate this code of conduct.

To report a concern privately, contact the project maintainers.
